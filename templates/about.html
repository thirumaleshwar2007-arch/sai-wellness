About Sravanti & SAI Wellness

Meet Sravanti

Wellness Consultant • 9 Years Experience

With nearly a decade of experience in wellness consulting, Sravanti founded SAI Wellness Centre with a vision to create a community-based approach to health and nutrition. Her philosophy centers on the belief that true wellness comes from consistent, daily habits practiced in a supportive community environment.

Specializing in weight management, digestive wellness, and family nutrition, Sravanti has helped hundreds of individuals and families achieve their health goals through personalized guidance and nutritional support.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and families with the knowledge and support needed to achieve optimal health through natural nutrition and lifestyle modifications.

Our Vision

To build a healthier community where wellness is accessible, sustainable, and practiced together.
